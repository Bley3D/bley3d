About

Hands-on experience for your plastics projects

Technical expertise, hands-on production experience and a clear focus on solutions that are both practical and economical.

Experience across the plastics value chain

Since 2008, I have worked in the plastics industry across a wide range of roles – from production and process optimisation to project management, technical customer support, production management and technical sales.

My background is rooted in production, not just theory. I have supported injection-moulding processes, carried out tool trials, improved production workflows and managed projects from the initial technical enquiry through to series production.

My core areas are plastics engineering, injection moulding, CAD design and 3D printing. I look at a component not only from a design perspective, but also in terms of manufacturability, tooling concept, cost and suitability for future series production.

Today, I bring this experience together at BLEY 3D & Kunststofftechnik, supporting companies, trades and private customers with practical technical solutions – from the first idea and CAD design through to prototypes and preparation for possible series production.

My areas of expertise

  • Plastics engineering and injection moulding
  • Design and component development
  • 3D printing and prototypes
  • Tool sampling and validation
  • Production management and process optimization
  • Project management and technical project support
  • Tooling and component costing
  • Manufacturability and series-production assessment
  • Technical sales and customer support
  • Supplier and partner coordination

Also for private customers

Have your own idea, need a custom bracket for the workshop or home, or is just one small plastic component broken? BLEY 3D & Kunststofftechnik also supports private customers. Existing parts can be measured and recreated in CAD, or completely new solutions can be developed. Where a repair is technically sensible, replacing the entire product can often be avoided.
