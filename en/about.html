Practical experience for your plastics projects

Technical know-how, hands-on experience and a clear focus on feasible and economical solutions.

Practical knowledge across the entire value chain

Since 2008 I have worked in the plastics industry and gained experience in different areas across the entire value chain – from production and process optimization to project management, technical customer support, production management and technical sales.

My practical background means I understand requirements not only in theory but directly from production. I have supported injection-molding processes, sampled tools, optimized production workflows and managed projects from technical enquiry through to series implementation.

My focus is on plastics engineering, injection molding, design and 3D printing. I assess a component not only from a design perspective, but also in terms of manufacturability, tooling concept, cost and later series production.

Today I combine this experience at BLEY 3D & Plastics Engineering to support companies, trades and private customers in developing and implementing practical technical solutions – from the initial idea through design and prototypes to preparation for potential series production.

My areas of expertise

  • Plastics engineering and injection molding
  • Design and component development
  • 3D printing and prototypes
  • Tool sampling and validation
  • Production management and process optimization
  • Project management and technical project support
  • Tooling and component costing
  • Assessment of manufacturability and series implementation
  • Technical sales and customer consulting
  • Supplier and partner coordination

Also for private customers

Do you have your own idea, need a special bracket for the workshop or home, or has only a small plastic part of a product broken? You can also contact BLEY 3D & Plastics Engineering. Existing parts can be measured and redesigned, or individual solutions can be newly developed. Where a repair is technically viable, replacing the entire product can often be avoided.
