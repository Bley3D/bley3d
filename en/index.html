Technical solutions instead of standard parts

Whether you need a replacement part, a new product idea or production equipment, I support you from CAD design to the 3D-printed component. For small and medium injection-molded series, I coordinate tooling and part production through external manufacturing partners. For large series, I can connect you with suitable production partners.

01

CAD & Design

Design based on sketches, drawings, specifications or existing samples. Modification and optimization of existing components.

02

3D Printing

Individual parts, brackets, adapters, housings, fixtures, functional samples, prototypes and small series.

03

Replacement Parts from Samples

No longer available? An existing or damaged part can be measured, redesigned and manufactured again.

04

Prototypees

Fast functional samples to check geometry, assembly and application before later series production.

05

Tooling & Injection Molding

For small and medium series, I plan and coordinate injection-molding tools and subsequent part production through external manufacturing partners. For large series, I connect customers with suitable series suppliers. Toolmaking and injection molding are not performed directly by BLEY 3D & Plastics Engineering.

06

Companies & Private Customers

From a technical product idea to a small replacement part for home use: support for companies, trades and private customers. Custom brackets, adapters, repair solutions and one-off parts are also possible.

Also for private customers

Small part broken? Do not replace the whole product.

Ist an einem Gerät, Möbelstück, Fahrzeug oder in der Werkstatt nur ein kleines Kunststoffteil defekt oder nicht mehr erhältlich, kann ich das vorhandene Teil vermessen, in CAD nachkonstruieren und – wenn technisch sinnvoll – im 3D Printing neu fertigen. Auch eigene Ideen, spezielle Halterungen, Adapter oder individuelle Lösungen können umgesetzt werden. Das kann im Einzelfall Kosten sparen und vermeidet unnötigen Ersatz kompletter Produkte.
