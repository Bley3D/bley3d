7. Visitor statistics with GoatCounter

This website uses GoatCounter for privacy-friendly statistical reach measurement. In particular, page views and referrers are recorded and, where enabled in GoatCounter, aggregated information such as browser, operating system, language, screen width and approximate country. The data is evaluated statistically and is not combined into personal user profiles.

According to the provider, GoatCounter does not use tracking cookies, LocalStorage or persistent tracking IDs. To recognize repeat visits, the site identifier, IP address and user agent are technically assigned to a session and held in memory for a maximum of eight hours. The IP address and full user agent are not stored permanently in the database or on disk. Further information can be found in GoatCounter's privacy policy and in its GDPR information.

The legal basis for reach measurement is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is to understand the use and reach of this website in a privacy-friendly way and to improve the site technically and editorially.
