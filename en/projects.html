Project 01 · Replacement Part & 3D Printing

Phone holder – ball joint redesigned and 3D-printed

The ball joint of the existing phone holder was broken. The damaged component was redesigned in CAD using the original as a reference and then reproduced by 3D printing. After assembly, the existing phone holder could be used again.

Project 02 · Camping & 3D Printing

Rainwater drainage for a motorhome awning

During heavy rain, water ran directly down the extended awning or awning leg and collected at the pitch. A suitable collection and drainage solution was therefore designed and manufactured by 3D printing. Rainwater is collected at the component and directed several metres away from the motorhome through an attached hose, significantly reducing water accumulation directly next to the vehicle.
